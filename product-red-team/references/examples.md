# Authored Examples

These are illustrative outputs written for this skill, not model-run results.

## Complete case: automatic refund policy

**Input:** “Automatically refund every delivery more than 30 minutes late to reduce support contacts.”

**Illustrative output:** Strongest case: a clear rule can reduce customer effort. Priority issue: lateness may be measured after a customer-caused delay or an inaccurate estimate, creating unfair refunds and incentives. Mitigation: define an authoritative delivery timestamp, exclude known exceptions, and test a reversible pilot. Evidence needed: current contact reasons and share of late deliveries attributable to each cause.

## Difficult case: a persuasive but thin claim

**Input:** “Three interview quotes prove managers need a new analytics dashboard.”

**Illustrative output:** The quotes may reveal a decision-support problem but do not prove a dashboard is the right intervention. Review the manager decisions, existing data access, representativeness, and alternatives such as alerts or workflow changes before committing.
