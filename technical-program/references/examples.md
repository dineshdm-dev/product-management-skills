# Authored Examples

These are illustrative outputs written for this skill, not model-run results.

## Complete case: school transport change

**Input:** “A district will introduce a new bus-route assignment process before term starts. Families need notifications; staff need exception handling.”

**Illustrative output:** Objective: publish reliable assignments before term start with a controlled path for exceptions. Milestones: approve route rules; validate assignments on a representative sample; notify a limited cohort; open exception review; expand after accuracy and support checks. Dependencies include student address quality and notification consent. Decision gate: do not publish until exception ownership and correction timing are defined. Recovery: retain the prior assignment view and a manual escalation path.

## Difficult case: invented dates

**Input:** “Give me a launch plan for a migration.” No date, team, data volume, or rollback constraints are provided.

**Illustrative output:** Use phases rather than dates: mapping approval, dry run, validation review, controlled release, monitoring, and remediation window. Name missing volume, active-record behavior, and rollback options as planning assumptions rather than commitments.
