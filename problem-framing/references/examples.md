# Authored Examples

These are illustrative outputs written for this skill, not model-run results.

## Complete case: delayed clinic intake

**Input:** “Build a status dashboard because patients wait too long after submitting intake forms. Staff say missing documents often hold appointments.”

**Illustrative output:** The visible request is a dashboard; the framed problem is that staff and patients cannot identify or resolve the next blocking intake action before an appointment. The useful unit is an intake case, not form submissions. Known facts: delays and missing documents are reported. Assumption: staff can identify a responsible party and missing item. Non-goal: measuring staff productivity. Next step: map the case states and decision owner before choosing a dashboard.

## Difficult case: a premature cause

**Input:** “Returns are rising, so we need an AI assistant to stop fraud.” No evidence accompanies the claim.

**Illustrative output:** Rising returns is a symptom, not proof of fraud. Possible causes include product defects, policy confusion, fulfillment error, or abuse. Do not select an AI solution yet. First obtain return reasons, affected products, policy exceptions, and the decision that a reviewer currently makes.
