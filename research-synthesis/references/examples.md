# Authored Examples

These are illustrative outputs written for this skill, not model-run results.

## Complete case: library reservation notes

**Input:** Two librarians report patrons miss pickup deadlines; one says reminder emails arrive too late; a survey of 12 patrons includes four comments about unclear pickup locations and two about reminders.

**Illustrative output:** Evidence suggests two possible friction points: timing of reminders and location clarity. The librarians' observations and six patron comments do not establish prevalence across all patrons. The findings support testing earlier reminders and clearer location information; they do not support claiming that email is the primary cause. Missing evidence: reservation timestamps, delivery timing, and non-response reasons.

## Difficult case: conflicting sources

**Input:** One large customer requests bulk export; four support tickets request faster search; a sales note says exports are a deal-breaker.

**Illustrative output:** Preserve the conflict. Bulk export may matter to a high-value account, while search appears in more reported support cases. The material does not establish priority without customer value, affected workflow, and effort. Recommend a decision-specific follow-up, not a blended consensus.
