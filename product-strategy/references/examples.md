# Authored Examples

These are illustrative outputs written for this skill, not model-run results.

## Complete case: launch sequence

**Input:** “A local grocery delivery service can improve substitution recommendations or add business accounts. Drivers report substitution disputes; three small offices asked for recurring orders.”

**Illustrative output:** Prioritize substitution clarity first. It improves an existing high-frequency workflow and may reduce disputes across the current customer base. Business accounts could be attractive, but the evidence is limited to three requests and implies billing, fulfillment, and support changes. Tradeoff: defer a possible segment expansion. This recommendation would change if offices show materially higher repeat demand or substitution disputes are rare. Next step: measure dispute frequency and interview the offices about order cadence and payment needs.

## Difficult case: false precision

**Input:** “Score these five roadmap ideas from 1–100 and choose the winner.” The only input is feature names.

**Illustrative output:** A score would imply evidence that is absent. Compare the ideas only after naming target users, problem severity, strategic role, dependencies, and opportunity cost. If a choice is required now, prioritize the smallest reversible experiment that reveals whether a high-value customer problem exists.
